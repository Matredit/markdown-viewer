Markdown Cheatsheet A guide for Google Docs & Word users
⚠️ The 6 Non-Intuitive Rules (Common Word / Docs Traps)
Rule 1 Single Enter Is Just a Space
In Word, pressing Enter creates a new line. In Markdown, a single Enter is treated like a space. To create a new paragraph, press Enter twice (leave an empty line).
❌ Line 1[Enter]Line 2 → renders as "Line 1 Line 2"
✅ Line 1[Enter][Enter]Line 2 → renders as 2 paragraphs
Rule 2 Space After # and - Is Mandatory
You cannot attach words directly to formatting characters. Always put a space after #, -, 1., and >.
❌ #My Title or -List Item (treated as plain text)
✅ # My Title and - List Item
Rule 3 Formatting Tags Must Touch the Words
Do not leave spaces inside asterisks or underscores. The syntax must wrap the words snugly.
❌ ** bold text ** or * italic *
✅ **bold text** and *italic*
Rule 4 Checkboxes Need Strict Spacing
To create a checkbox, you need: dash + space + bracket + (space or x) + bracket + space.
❌ -[x] or -[] or [ ] (missing spaces)
✅ - [x] Done and - [ ] Pending task
Rule 5 Tables Require the Dash Line (---)
Pipes | alone don't make a table. Line 2 must have dashes |---|---| to separate headers from data rows.
❌ | Header 1 | Header 2 | followed by | Data 1 | Data 2 |
✅ | Header 1 | Header 2 |
   | :--- | :--- |
   | Data 1 | Data 2 |
Rule 6 Triple Backticks Belong on Their Own Line
Opening ```lang and closing ``` must be on their own separate lines, never merged with code.
❌ ```print("hello")```
✅ ```python
   print("hello")
   ```
